Welcome to WARP⚡FALL SMP

Your complete guide to surviving, building, questing, trading and exploring Warpfall.

Start Playing

Start Here

Enter the lobby, use the Wilderness NPC to reach the normal survival world, then use RTP to find your own adventure. Start gathering resources, make a base, set a home and meet other players.

  • Explore and claim your own spot.
  • Use /sethome so you can return safely.
  • Use quests and EliteMobs for extra progression.
  • Use the shop and Auction House to build your economy.

What Warpfall Has

Vanilla+ survival with RPG features, quests, custom mobs, economy, NPCs, MineChess, graves, VeinMiner, cross-platform support and more.

Gameplay

Survival

Gather resources, build, explore, fight and create your own base. Keep a home set so you always have a reliable return point.

Quests

Talk to quest NPCs and complete BeautyQuests objectives for rewards and progression.

EliteMobs

Watch for stronger RPG-style enemies and special encounters. Fight carefully and use good gear.

Graves

When configured, Graves can protect your dropped items after death. Return to your grave and recover your belongings.

MineChess

Take a break from survival and challenge other players to MineChess.

RTP

Use the Wilderness flow or /rtp where enabled to find a random survival location.

Economy & Trading

Balance

Check your money with /balance.

Server Shop

Use EconomyShopGUI to buy and sell supported items.

Auction House

Use /ah to browse player listings and sell your own items through the Auction House.

Essential Commands

/spawn Return to spawn/lobby.

/rtp Random teleport where enabled.

/sethome Save a home.

/home Return to your home.

/tpa player Request to teleport to a player.

/tpaccept Accept a teleport request.

/tpdeny Deny a teleport request.

/balance Check your money.

/ah Open Auction House.

/msg player message Private message.

/r message Reply to the last private message.

/help View available help.

/rules View server rules.

Ranks & Chat

Warpfall uses LuckPerms ranks with TAB/LPC presentation. Your rank affects how your name and chat are displayed. Staff ranks are reserved for trusted members of the server team.

Java, Bedrock & Older Versions

Warpfall uses Geyser/Floodgate for Bedrock connectivity and ViaVersion/ViaBackwards for version compatibility. Compatibility does not guarantee that every client version can display or use every new Minecraft feature exactly like the server's native version.

Voice Chat

Simple Voice Chat is an optional planned feature. Once it is enabled on the server, install the matching client mod and use the in-game voice controls. Players should use the official project download listed by staff.

Rules & Safety

  • Do not grief, steal or destroy another player's builds without permission.
  • Do not cheat, exploit bugs or use prohibited clients.
  • Do not harass, threaten or spam other players.
  • Respect staff instructions and other players.
  • Report serious issues to staff instead of starting arguments.

FAQ

How do I get into survival?

Start in the lobby and use the Wilderness NPC. Follow the teleport/RTP flow into the normal survival world.

How do I get back?

Use /home for your saved base or /spawn when you need to return to spawn.

How do I make money?

Sell items through the server shop, trade with players and list valuable items on Auction House.

What if I die?

Check for your grave and return to it to recover your items, depending on the current GravesX configuration.

Can Bedrock players join?

Geyser/Floodgate are installed for Bedrock support. Connection details should be provided by the server staff.

What if a command does not work?

Check /help, make sure you have the correct permissions, and ask staff if the feature is unavailable.
